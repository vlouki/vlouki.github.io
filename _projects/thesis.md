## Overview

**Myhua** is a full-stack web application developed to streamline the management of **university committees**, meetings, and administrative workflows at Harokopio University of Athens. The project was initiated to address the **university's need** for a centralized solution, as no dedicated system previously existed. Designed to integrate with the university's existing infrastructure, myHUA brings together essential administrative processes and information into a single, efficient, and user-friendly platform.
