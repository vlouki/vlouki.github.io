Hello, I'm Vyronas Loukidelis, a graduate of the [Department of Informatics and Telematics](https://dit.hua.gr/index.php/en) at Harokopio University of Athens, eager to take my first steps in the IT industry.

Throughout my studies, I have had the opportunity to explore and get involved in various areas of computer science, building a solid foundation in:

- **Software Development & Object-Oriented Programming** (Python, Java, C/C++)
- **Web Application Technologies**
- **Relational Databases & SQL**
- **Decision Support Systems & Data Analysis** (NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib)
- **Android Mobile Application Development**
- **DevOps & Deployment Automation** (Git, Ansible, Docker, Jenkins, CI/CD, Kubernetes)
- **Cryptography & Cybersecurity**
- **Cloud Computing & Distributed Systems**
- **Quantum Computing Fundamentals** (Qubits, Superposition, Entanglement, Quantum Algorithms)

Through academic projects, I have been able to apply my knowledge, collaborate with others, develop practical skills, and gain a better understanding of different fields of technology.

As I begin my professional journey, I am motivated to keep learning, set new goals and take on new challenges.

## The Person Behind the Code

Outside of technology, sports have always been a big part of my life. I'm an amateur football player and enjoy running, staying active and challenging myself physically.

I also love traveling, exploring new places and experiencing different cultures.

On the creative side, I recently started rug tufting, designing and creating handmade rugs from scratch. It's a hobby that allows me to express my creativity and work on something completely different from the digital world.
